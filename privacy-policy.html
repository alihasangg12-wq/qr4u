Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

QR4U ("qr4u.site", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website and use our custom QR code generator.

2. Browser-Based QR Generation & Logo Uploads

  • QR Content: URLs, Wi-Fi details, vCard contact fields, WhatsApp messages, text, SMS, email drafts, and phone numbers you enter into the generator are used locally in your browser to render your QR preview and export PNG or SVG files.
  • Uploaded Logos: When you select a custom logo image (JPG, JPEG, or PNG), it is read locally in your browser via the FileReader API so it can be drawn onto your QR canvas preview.
  • No Mandatory Accounts: You can generate and download QR codes without creating an account.
