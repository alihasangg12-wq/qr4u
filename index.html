Free Custom QR Code Generator with Optional Frames & Center Logos

QR4U gives you a clean, ready-to-scan QR code immediately on load—without forcing a frame or center logo unless you choose to add one. When you do want extra branding, you can switch between separate Frame and Logo sections, customize colors, and export as PNG or SVG.

How to Use the Generator

  1. Pick a QR type & enter details: Choose Website URL, Wi-Fi, vCard, WhatsApp, Social, PDF Link, Plain Text, SMS, Email, or Phone.
  2. Open the Frame tab (optional): Choose a frame style such as Envelope, Classic Banner, Sketch, Mug, Scooter, or Coffee Cup and customize label text and colors.
  3. Open the Logo tab (optional): Pick a preset icon (Scan Me, WhatsApp, Link, Location, Wi-Fi, Contact, Email, Menu, PayPal, Bitcoin) or upload your own JPG/PNG logo.
  4. Download PNG or SVG: Save your finished QR code for digital sharing or crisp print layouts.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are frames and logos selected by default when I open the page?
No. The generator starts with No Frame and No Logo selected so you only add them when you want.

2. Can I upload my own brand logo?
Yes. Go to the Logo section and click or drag-and-drop any JPG, JPEG, or PNG image up to 2MB.

3. Do static QR codes expire?
No. Static QR codes encode your information directly into the QR pattern and keep working without expiration.

4. Should I choose PNG or SVG?
Use PNG for websites, social posts, and messaging, and SVG for scalable printing.
