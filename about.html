About QR4U

Simple, Fast & Privacy-Friendly QR Code Creation

QR4U (qr4u.org) helps creators, businesses, shops, and everyday users generate clean custom QR codes in seconds without complicated sign-ups.

Our Mission

Many QR tools lock basic customization behind paywalls or force clutter onto your code before you even start. QR4U is built around a clean workflow: start with a plain, scannable QR code, and add optional frames or center logos only when they match your design.

Browser-Based Generation

QR codes are generated directly in your browser for quick live previews and instant downloads.

Optional Frames & Logos

Choose from clean frames—including delivery scooter, classic banner, mug, and coffee cup—or add preset and custom logos.

PNG & SVG Ready

Export raster PNGs for screens and social posts or SVG files for scalable print materials.

What You Can Create

  • Links & Media: Website URLs, social profiles, and public PDF links.
  • Instant Connection: Wi-Fi network credentials, vCard contact cards, WhatsApp chats, SMS, email drafts, and direct phone numbers.
  • Custom Styling: Custom foreground, background, and label colors with live preview updates.
